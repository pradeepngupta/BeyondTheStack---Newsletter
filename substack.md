### 📌 Content Strategy

* Pick **3–4 high-performing LinkedIn posts** (your best value posts).

* Add a top line:
  > 🕰 *Originally published on LinkedIn. Brought here for new readers.*
  >
* Maintain a **weekly or biweekly cadence** — consistency > volume.

### 📢 Announce the Move

* Write a  **Post Launch Edition** :
  * Why you’re moving.
  * What readers will get (clean reading experience, early access, full editions).
  * Invitation to  **subscribe** .
* Post a **LinkedIn teaser** with:
  * ✍️ 2–3 line hook.
  * 🔗 Substack link.
  * 🎯 CTA: “Subscribe to get full editions.”

### 🪄 Cross-Posting Flow (Per Post)

1. **Publish on Substack** (main home).
2. **Post teaser on LinkedIn** (hook + CTA).
3. **Cross-post on Medium** for SEO and discoverability.
4. Add “Originally published on Substack” line to Medium.

🎯  *Outcome* : Readers understand that Substack is the **primary home** for your work.



### 🧭 **✍️📆 Interleaved Phase — “Live Publishing Loop”**

#### 🧠 **1. Set Your Rhythm**

* **Frequency:** Weekly or biweekly (pick one and stick to it — consistency matters more than volume).
* **Format:** Keep a familiar structure so your readers instantly recognize your editions. Example:
  * Intro hook 🪝
  * Main insight 💡
  * Practical takeaway ⚡
  * Optional CTA (e.g., “Subscribe for more deep dives”)


#### 🏡 **2. Substack Is Your Home Base**

* Draft and publish each **new edition** on Substack first.
* Use:
  * A **clear title** with your “Edition numbering” → e.g.,

    `Edition #6 — Scaling Like Netflix`
  * A featured image or banner (optional but great for branding).
  * Tagging and categories (to build structure for your archive).

---

#### 📣 **3. Tease on LinkedIn (Same Day)**

* Post a **short teaser** or a **1-paragraph summary** on LinkedIn.
* End with a line like:

  👉 *“Read the full edition on Substack → [link]”*
* This drives your **LinkedIn audience to subscribe** on Substack.

🧠 *Pro tip:* Hook with a question or stat to increase click-through:

> “How does Netflix scale to millions of concurrent users?
>
> I broke down their approach in my latest newsletter 👉 [link]”

---

#### 🌐 **4. Optional Medium Cross-Post** *(24–48h later)*

* Paste the same content on Medium.
* Add a note at the top like:

  📝 *“Originally published in my newsletter ‘Beyond the Stack’. Subscribe for early access.”*
* This improves SEO + gives your content a  **second life** .

---

#### 🧭 **5. Keep a Simple Cadence**

Here’s a  **sample weekly flow** :

| Day       | Task                                       | Platform          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| Monday    | Publish full edition                       | Substack          |
| Monday    | Teaser post                                | LinkedIn          |
| Wednesday | Cross-post                                 | Medium            |
| Friday    | Optional engagement (e.g., comment, polls) | LinkedIn/Substack |

---

#### 📈 **6. Compound Effect**

* As you do this weekly, each new edition builds:
  * Archive depth 📚
  * Consistency 🧭
  * Reader trust 🤝
  * SEO & discoverability 🌍

---

👉 **Why this phase is powerful:**

* Your Substack isn’t just a copy of your LinkedIn — it becomes your  **primary publishing platform** .
* You keep your audience engaged **while migrating archives in parallel** (Phase 3).
* It **trains your readers** to expect new content from Substack first.

## 🧠 **Phase 3 — Archive Expansion (Drip Republish)**
