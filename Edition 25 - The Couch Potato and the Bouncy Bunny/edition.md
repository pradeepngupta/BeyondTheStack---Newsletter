# **The Couch Potato and the Bouncy Bunny — A System Design Story**

A fun, story-driven analogy that explains lazy evaluation vs. eager execution in large-scale systems.


🔹 We opened with a playful metaphor — comparing caching strategies to lifestyle choices. It wasn’t just fun; it unpacked the **importance of performance-aware design** using analogies anyone can remember.


Story:

Once upon a time, in a cozy little house, there lived a friendly but very lazy
potato named Pat. Pat was not an ordinary potato; he was a couch potato! Instead
of rolling around and having fun outside, Pat loved to sit on the comfy couch all
day long, watching TV and playing video games.
One sunny day, Pat’s friend, a bouncy bunny named Bella, came over to visit. Bella
hopped in with a big smile and said, “Hi, Pat! Let’s go outside and play! There are
so many fun things to do!”
But Pat just yawned and said, “Oh, Bella, I’m too tired. I just want to stay here
on the couch.”
Bella’s ears perked up with excitement. “But there’s a big park with swings,
slides, and a fun treasure hunt waiting for us! Don’t you want to come?”
Pat thought for a moment. He had never been on the swings or explored the park
before. Maybe it would be fun! With a little wiggle and a stretch, Pat finally got
off the couch. Bella and Pat went to the park, and Pat had the best day ever! He
swung high, slid fast, and found hidden treasures.
When the sun began to set, Pat and Bella walked back home, and Pat felt very
happy. He realized that sometimes it’s great to leave the couch and go on
adventures. From that day on, Pat made sure to spend more time playing and
exploring, and he still enjoyed a little bit of couch time now and then.
And so, Pat learned that while it’s nice to relax, it’s also fun to get up and move
around. And Bella was always there to remind him that the world outside was full
of exciting things waiting to be discovered!
